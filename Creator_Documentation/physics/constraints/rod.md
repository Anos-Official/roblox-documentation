# Rod

English

[Edit](https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/blob/main/content/en-us/physics/constraints/rod.md)

For an overview on creating, visualizing, and simulating mechanical constraints, including [RodConstraint](/docs/en-us/reference/engine/classes/RodConstraint) , see [Mechanical constraints](/docs/en-us/physics/mechanical-constraints) . Also see [Roblox units](/docs/en-us/physics/units)  to understand how Roblox units compare to metric units.

A [RodConstraint](/docs/en-us/reference/engine/classes/RodConstraint)  keeps two attachments separated by its defined [Length](/docs/en-us/reference/engine/classes/RodConstraint#Length) . By default, both attachments can rotate freely, although you can enable limits to restrict rotational tilt.

## [Limits](#limits)[](#limits)

You can limit rotation of the attachments within a cone, independently of each other, by enabling the [LimitsEnabled](/docs/en-us/reference/engine/classes/RodConstraint#LimitsEnabled)  property and setting [LimitAngle0](/docs/en-us/reference/engine/classes/RodConstraint#LimitAngle0)  and [LimitAngle1](/docs/en-us/reference/engine/classes/RodConstraint#LimitAngle1)  respectively.
