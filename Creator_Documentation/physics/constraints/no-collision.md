# NoCollision

English

[Edit](https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/blob/main/content/en-us/physics/constraints/no-collision.md)

The [NoCollisionConstraint](/docs/en-us/reference/engine/classes/NoCollisionConstraint)  prevents collisions between two specific parts, but those parts may still register collisions with the rest of the world. Compared to [collision groups](/docs/en-us/workspace/collisions#collision-groups) , it provides a direct way to disable specific collisions, such as the wheel of a car scraping against the car's body.
