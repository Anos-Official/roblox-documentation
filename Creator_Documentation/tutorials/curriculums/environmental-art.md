# Environmental art curriculum

English

[Edit](https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/blob/main/content/en-us/tutorials/curriculums/environmental-art/index.md)

Environmental art  is the discipline of creating and configuring 3D environments that embody and facilitate gameplay requirements, immerse users within your game, and provide contextual information about the world itself.

You'll learn how to recreate a [high-quality environment](https://www.roblox.com/games/14447845297/Environment-Art-Optimizing)  for a first-person shooter laser tag game by following step-by-step processes and best practices for level design, developing assets, and optimization efforts for memory and performance that you can then implement in your own games.

This course is intended for readers who are familiar with general building concepts and tools in Roblox Studio. If you need help learning how to build an environment, try the [core curriculum](/docs/en-us/tutorials/curriculums/core) .

## [Course contents](#course-contents)[](#course-contents)

Chapter 1 - Greybox your environment

Learn how to [add simple shapes to the 3D space](/docs/en-us/tutorials/curriculums/environmental-art/greybox-your-environment)  in order to figure out how users will experience gameplay before investing time into polishing assets.

[Get the Place File](https://www.roblox.com/games/14447721254/Environment-Art-Greyboxing)

Chapter 2 - Develop polished assets

Learn about the concepts behind [designing high-quality assets](/docs/en-us/tutorials/curriculums/environmental-art/develop-polished-assets)  in third-party modeling tools that replace the greybox environment.

[Get the Asset Library](https://www.roblox.com/library/14447738661/Environment-Art-Asset-Library)

Chapter 3 - Assemble an asset library

Learn how to [import and configure your assets](/docs/en-us/tutorials/curriculums/environmental-art/assemble-an-asset-library)  in a central location within your game for easy access and reuse.

[Get the Place File](https://www.roblox.com/games/14447787049/Environment-Art-Assembling)

Chapter 4 - Construct your world

Learn how to [utilize your asset library](/docs/en-us/tutorials/curriculums/environmental-art/construct-your-world) , as well as how to add and configure additional elements of the 3D space to bring your world to life.

[Get the Place File](https://www.roblox.com/games/14447826396/Environment-Art-Constructing)

Chapter 5 - Optimize your game

Learn different strategies to [keep your frame rate and performance levels high](/docs/en-us/tutorials/curriculums/environmental-art/optimize-your-experience)  across devices, especially those with memory limitations.

[Get the Place File](https://www.roblox.com/games/14447845297/Environment-Art-Optimizing)
