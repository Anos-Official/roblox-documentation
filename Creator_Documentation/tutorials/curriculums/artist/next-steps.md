Congratulations on completing your first VFX art with Roblox Studio! Now that you have experience creating custom special effects with unique visuals and behavior, you can share your art with others and extend your skills by learning about Studio's additional VFX features.

## [Share with others](#share-with-others)[](#share-with-others)

When you first publish a game, it's only playable by you as the creator. However, creating on Roblox is always more fun when you can share what you make with others. For steps on inviting early testers to preview your game, see [Invite early testers](/docs/en-us/production/publishing/publish-games-and-places#invite-early-testers) . For steps on how to release your game to players, see [Release games](/docs/en-us/production/publishing/publish-games-and-places#release-games) .

## [Continue learning](#continue-learning)[](#continue-learning)

Particle emitters are an excellent start for creating VFX, but Studio offers many additional VFX features to boost the visuals of your games, including:

-

[Beams](/docs/en-us/effects/beams)  that render a texture between two points that stay still.

-

[Trails](/docs/en-us/effects/trails)  that render a texture between two points that move.

-

[Flipbooks](/docs/en-us/effects/particle-emitters#flipbooks)  that animate a texture over time.

-

[Light sources](/docs/en-us/effects/light-sources)  that emit light at specific points in the 3D space.
