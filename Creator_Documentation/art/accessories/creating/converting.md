# Convert

English

[Edit](https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/blob/main/content/en-us/art/accessories/creating/converting.md)

With the [Model](/docs/en-us/reference/engine/classes/Model)  in your project, the last step in the process of clothing creation requires you to convert this object to a standard [Accessory](/docs/en-us/reference/engine/classes/Accessory)  that avatars can equip. Using the [Accessory Fitting Tool](/docs/en-us/avatar/accessory-fitting-tool)  (AFT), convert the model object to a [Accessory](/docs/en-us/reference/engine/classes/Accessory)  that you can then use in your game or publish to the Marketplace.

To generate the accessory object:

1.

In the Avatar  tab, click Accessory  to open the AFT. The Accessory Fitting Tool  panel displays.

2.

In the panel:

1.

Select the Part  field, then in the Explorer  window, select the layered accessory [MeshPart](/docs/en-us/reference/engine/classes/MeshPart)  object.

2.

Back in the panel, click the Next  button. The Asset Type  page displays.

3.

In the Asset Type  page:

1.

Set asset type to Clothing , then use the dropdown menu to select the clothing type. This tutorial sets it to Shirt .

2.

Click the Next  button. A preview panel displays with a default character wearing your layered accessory.

4.

Test out various sample characters, clothing, and animations. If necessary, make minor cage adjustments using the editing features, or larger cage changes back in your third-party modeling software. For more information on this process, see [Accessory Fitting Tool - Test accessories](/docs/en-us/avatar/accessory-fitting-tool#test-accessories) .

5.

After previewing your asset, click the Generate MeshPart Accessory  button. Your 3D model populates in your project as an [Accessory](/docs/en-us/reference/engine/classes/Accessory)  object.

![](https://prod.docsiteassets.roblox.com/assets/art/accessories/creating/Exporting-Clothing-in-Studio-Highlight.png) Clothing accessory in viewport

![](https://prod.docsiteassets.roblox.com/assets/art/accessories/creating/Exporting-Accessory-Explorer.png) Clothing accessory in Explorer

Congratulations, you've completed the layered accessory tutorial. With your new [Accessory](/docs/en-us/reference/engine/classes/Accessory) , you can perform any of the following:

-

[Upload the accessory](/docs/en-us/art/accessories/creating-rigid/publishing)  to the Marketplace.

-

Use the accessory in your current game by equipping it to character models with a [HumanoidDescription](/docs/en-us/characters/appearance#manually-modify-appearance) , or by dragging and dropping the accessory under the appropriate character [Model](/docs/en-us/reference/engine/classes/Model)  object.

-

Save the accessory to your [Toolbox](/docs/en-us/projects/assets/toolbox)  to share or use within any of your games.
