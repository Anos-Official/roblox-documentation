# Sculpt detail

English

[Edit](https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/blob/main/content/en-us/art/accessories/creating/sculpting.md)

After scaling and positioning your t-shirt mesh, begin applying sculpting detail to add cloth and fabric details. While there are many ways to sculpt an object in Blender, this tutorial primarily uses the Elastic Deform , Inflate , and Clothing  tools to make your mesh look more like a realistic t-shirt.

![](https://prod.docsiteassets.roblox.com/assets/art/accessories/creating/Modeling-Complete-2.png) Clothing mesh after sculpting details

To add clothing details:

1.

With LongSleeve  active, switch to Sculpt  mode.

2.

In the top right corner of the viewport:

1.

Disable Wireframe  mode.

2.

Enable X-Axis symmetry  to perform symmetrical edits.

3.

Select the Elastic Deform  tool at .5  strength to stretch parts of vertices to completely cover the mannequin.

1.

Use F  to change the radius of the brush.

2.

Hide the mannequin to access hard-to-reach areas.

4.

Select the Cloth  tool, then:

1.

Disable X-Axis symmetry . The Cloth  tool can produce unexpected results with symmetry enabled.

2.

Click and drag on your mesh to add a cloth-like surface to your mesh. Adjust the settings to change the strength of the deformations.

5.

Using the Elastic Deform , Inflate , and Cloth  tools, make the final adjustments to your mesh so that it sits on top of the mannequin with the final desired shape.
