# Avatar items

English

[Edit](https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/blob/main/content/en-us/production/monetization/avatar-items.md)

Avatar items  are 3D assets, such as accessories and clothing, that Roblox avatars can equip and wear. While many avatar items are sold on the Roblox's Marketplace, you can work with item creators to sell avatar items exclusively in your game.

By selling unique avatar items in your game, you can create game-specific cosmetics while still taking advantage of Roblox's [commission payouts](/docs/marketplace/marketplace-fees-and-commissions#commissions)  for avatar assets sold within a game.

 ![](https://prod.docsiteassets.roblox.com/assets/monetization/avatar-items/Linked-Avatar-Items-Example.png)

For information on how to create and sell your own avatar items, which requires third-party software such as Blender or Maya, see Roblox's [Avatar](/docs/avatar)  documentation.

## [Enable in-game sales](#enable-in-game-sales)[](#enable-in-game-sales)

To set an avatar item on sale, the item creator and the game owner must perform the following:

1.

The item creator  must [add the game's Place ID](#add-place-id)  to their item's sale location data.

2.

The game owner  must [add the avatar item's Asset ID](#add-items-to-game)  and enable the item.

1.

If the game owner and the item creator are the same person, Roblox automatically adds and enables the asset to the game after setting the sale location.

### [Add place ID](#add-place-id)[](#add-place-id)

Before enabling an avatar item sale in your game, the creator of the item must add the game's starting place ID to the avatar item's sales location.

1.

Open the [Creations](https://create.roblox.com/dashboard/creations)  page on Creator Dashboard .

2.

Click the ⋯  in the corner of the game's thumbnail and select Copy Start Place ID .

3.

As the item creator, [update your item's marketplace settings](/docs/marketplace/publish-to-marketplace#marketplace-settings)  and add the Place ID to the item's Sale Location field.

### [Add items to game](#add-items-to-game)[](#add-items-to-game)

After the item creator adds your game to the asset's sale location, you can now enable the item for the game.

To search and enable an asset for sale:

1.

Navigate to your [Creations](https://create.roblox.com/dashboard/creations)  page on Creator Dashboard  and select your game.

2.

In the Monetization  menu, select Avatar Items . All avatar items for that game display.

3.

Using the search bar, use the item's Asset ID to find the avatar item.

1.

If you do not see the item populate, verify that your Asset ID is correct and that the item creator has added the correct Place ID to the item's sales locations.

4.

Using the dropdown on the thumbnail, click Enable Sale .

![](https://prod.docsiteassets.roblox.com/assets/monetization/avatar-items/Avatar-Items-Enable.png)

## [Avatar items analytics](#avatar-items-analytics)[](#avatar-items-analytics)

Avatar Items analytics help you gauge the success of individual Avatar Items, identify trends, and forecast potential future earnings.

To access Avatar Items analytics:

1.

Navigate to your [Creations](https://create.roblox.com/dashboard/creations)  page on Creator Dashboard  and select your game.

2.

Navigate to Monetization  ⟩ Avatar Items  and select the Analytics  tab.

 ![](https://prod.docsiteassets.roblox.com/assets/monetization/avatar-items/avatar-items-analytics.png)

The analytics tab enables you to:

-

View top performing items:  See your top selling and top grossing Avatar Items over a selected time period.

-

Analyze overall sales and net revenue:  Showcase up to eight top items on a time-series graph.

-

Monitor your catalog:  Examine a table with up to 400 items, sortable by sales and net revenue.

-

View buyer demographics:  See the demographic information of buyers, including age, gender, location, and platform.

 ![](https://prod.docsiteassets.roblox.com/assets/monetization/avatar-items/avatar-items-analytics-2.png)  ![](https://prod.docsiteassets.roblox.com/assets/monetization/avatar-items/avatar-item-analytics-3.png)
