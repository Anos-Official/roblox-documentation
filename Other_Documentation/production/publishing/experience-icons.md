# Icons

English

[Edit](https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/blob/main/content/en-us/production/publishing/experience-icons.md)

A high-quality icon , in addition to a showcase [thumbnail](/docs/production/publishing/thumbnails) , is an important factor in helping users recognize your game and grow its brand. With prominent placement in various sections of the [Home](https://www.roblox.com/home)  page, it's recommended that the icon expresses the theme, tone, and/or genre of the game.

![Icon expressing an urban action game](https://prod.docsiteassets.roblox.com/assets/publishing/experience-metadata/Icon-Action.jpg) Urban action game

![Icon expressing a camping simulation](https://prod.docsiteassets.roblox.com/assets/publishing/experience-metadata/Icon-Camping.jpg) Camping simulation

![Icon expressing an ocean theme](https://prod.docsiteassets.roblox.com/assets/publishing/experience-metadata/Icon-Ocean.jpg) Peaceful ocean theme

When you [publish a game](/docs/production/publishing/publish-games-and-places)  for the first time, an icon is automatically generated from a collection of default images. While you can only display one icon per [locale](/docs/production/localization) , you can also include supplementary [image and video thumbnails](/docs/production/publishing/thumbnails)  that further promote the game.

For more information on maximizing the impact of your game's icon, see [best practices](#best-practices) . To update the icon with Open Cloud, see [Manage experience icons and thumbnails](/docs/cloud/guides/experience-icons-and-thumbnails#update-the-place-icon) .

## [Upload icons](#upload-icons)[](#upload-icons)

To ensure your game's icon always displays in high resolution and with an ideal aspect ratio, use a template of 512×512 pixels . In some places on the Roblox site and app, icons scale down to smaller sizes like 150×150 pixels, so it's recommended that you preview an icon at smaller sizes to confirm its details remain clear. In addition, see [best practices](#best-practices)  for tips on icon format and design.

To upload a game icon:

1.

Navigate to the [Creator Dashboard](https://create.roblox.com/dashboard/creations) .

2.

Click on the game you want to upload an icon for. The game's Overview  page displays.

3.

In the left-hand navigation, navigate to Configurations  > Places .

4.

Click the start place  marked with a star icon (if the game contains only one place, it will be the only option). The place's Basic Settings  page displays.

 ![Start place tile indicated in Places display on the Creator Dashboard](https://prod.docsiteassets.roblox.com/assets/creator-dashboard/Places-Start-Place-No-Icon.png)

5.

In the place's left-hand navigation menu, select Icon .

6.

Set the media type to Image  and click the Change  button. Then, from the file browser, select and confirm the image you want to use as the icon. Note that all icon images must pass [moderation](/docs/projects/assets#asset-moderation)  before they appear to others on the Roblox platform.

7.

If the preview icon appears as you expect, click Save Changes .

## [Best practices](#best-practices)[](#best-practices)

To create the most positive impact for users looking to play your game, consider the following best practices.

### [Quality and aspect ratio](#quality-and-aspect-ratio)[](#quality-and-aspect-ratio)

When you upload an icon image, it should be square  and at least 512×512 pixels  so that it always displays in high resolution and at an ideal aspect ratio across the Roblox site and app.

![High quality and sharp icon](https://prod.docsiteassets.roblox.com/assets/publishing/experience-metadata/Icon-High-Res.jpg)

High quality and sharp

![Low quality and blurry icon example](https://prod.docsiteassets.roblox.com/assets/publishing/experience-metadata/Icon-Low-Res.jpg)

Low quality and blurry

![Non-square icon](https://prod.docsiteassets.roblox.com/assets/publishing/experience-metadata/Icon-Not-Square.jpg)

Not a square image

### [Relevant content](#relevant-content)[](#relevant-content)

An icon will have a higher impact if it's unique and provides relevant imagery on what users should expect when they join the game. Icons with ambiguous graphics may lead to unnecessary confusion.

![Icon expressing a fun theme park setting](https://prod.docsiteassets.roblox.com/assets/publishing/experience-metadata/Icon-Theme-Park.jpg)

Icon expresses a fun theme park setting

![Default Roblox icon which does not match the same theme](https://prod.docsiteassets.roblox.com/assets/publishing/experience-metadata/Icon-Default.jpg)

Default icon does not match the same theme

![Icon with ambiguous graphic in top-right corner](https://prod.docsiteassets.roblox.com/assets/publishing/experience-metadata/Icon-Theme-Park-Symbol.jpg)

Ambiguous graphic in top‑right corner

### [Color and contrast](#color-and-contrast)[](#color-and-contrast)

You can express your game's theme through color and contrast to help users decide if it's suitable and appealing to them.

![Icon with bright, high-saturation colors for a fantasy or dreamy setting](https://prod.docsiteassets.roblox.com/assets/publishing/experience-metadata/Icon-Colorization-A.jpg) Bright, high-saturation colors for a fantasy or dreamy setting

![Icon with muted low-saturation colors for a somber, moody setting](https://prod.docsiteassets.roblox.com/assets/publishing/experience-metadata/Icon-Colorization-B.jpg) Muted low-saturation colors for a somber, moody setting

![Icon with heavy color balance and contrast for a horror game](https://prod.docsiteassets.roblox.com/assets/publishing/experience-metadata/Icon-Colorization-C.jpg) Heavy color balance and contrast for a horror game
