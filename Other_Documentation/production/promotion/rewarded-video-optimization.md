Rewarded Video Optimization Playbook

A quick-reference guide for creators who have already integrated rewarded video ads but are seeing low opt-in, low repeat usage, or soft earnings. Diagnose the likely cause behind a weak metric and apply the matching fix.

![](https://prod.docsiteassets.roblox.com/assets/monetization/rewarded-video-optimization/Icon-Checklist.png)

## Self-Audit Checklist

-

Is your RV prompt visible within the first couple of actions after the triggering moment, not buried in a menu?

-

Do you offer more than one reward type across the prompt?

-

Is your frequency cap at least 2-3 prompts per session (or an equivalent daily cap)?

-

Are your rewards consumable and repeatable rather than one-time durables?

-

Is your RV integrated within or near the core loop of the game?

Rule of thumb: diagnose from the metric that's lagging, then apply the matching fix below before testing new creative or ad network changes.

![Trails shop with a prompt to watch an ad for 2x egg growth for 5 minutes](https://prod.docsiteassets.roblox.com/assets/monetization/rewarded-video-optimization/user_journey.png)

## The user journey

01

### Core loop

Opt-in rewarded video fits at natural pauses, such as after a round, or at a checkpoint. It adds value without breaking flow.

02

### Friction points

When a player is out of currency or stuck, rewarded video keeps them engaged and earns developers revenue from players who don't buy.

03

### Return visits

Daily rewards and events are good moments to offer an opt-in bonus that makes coming back more rewarding.

### Clutch HUD button

Appears right after a near-death or low-resource moment, offering a clear reward.

![An ad button buried in a store submenu](https://prod.docsiteassets.roblox.com/assets/monetization/rewarded-video-optimization/buried_ad.png)

### Buried ad button

Ad button buried three taps deep in Store → Boosters → Ads.

![A bonus rewarded video revive option on an end-of-level screen](https://prod.docsiteassets.roblox.com/assets/monetization/rewarded-video-optimization/bonus_end.png)

### Bonus end-of-level

End-of-level screen offers a bonus RV option alongside standard rewards.

![A generic Watch Ad button with no visible reward](https://prod.docsiteassets.roblox.com/assets/monetization/rewarded-video-optimization/lack_of_context.png)

### Lack of context

Generic "Watch Ad" button with no context or visible reward.

![A rewarded video prompt that shows the reward value before the player opts in](https://prod.docsiteassets.roblox.com/assets/monetization/rewarded-video-optimization/clear_value.png)

### Clear value proposition

Reward value is shown before the player commits to watching.

![The same cash offer shown to a player who already has plenty of cash](https://prod.docsiteassets.roblox.com/assets/monetization/rewarded-video-optimization/no_player_differentiation.png)

### No player differentiation

Same placement shown regardless of whether the player actually needs it.

## RV Health Benchmarks & Fixes

| Metric | | Target | | How to Improve |
| --- | --- |
| Opt-in Rate | | Target 10-20%+ | | How to improve Surface RV prompts contextually (e.g. HUD notification at a moment of need) instead of burying them in menus. |
| Frequency | | Target 2-4+ | | How to improve Loosen caps where possible and offer consumable, repeatable rewards (boosts, currency, extra lives) that encourage re-engagement. |
| Imp/DAU | | Target 0.5+ | | How to improve Offer multiple reward options spanning new and existing player needs, and test reward value against perceived cost of watching. |
