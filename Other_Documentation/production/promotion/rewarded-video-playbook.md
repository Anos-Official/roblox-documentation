## What is Rewarded Video?

A rewarded video is a full-screen ad that lasts 6 to 30 seconds and rewards the user with an item. Rewarded ads are a win for users, creators, and advertisers.

![](https://prod.docsiteassets.roblox.com/assets/monetization/rewarded-video-ads/playbooks/Icon-Monetize.png)

### Monetize more of your players

Reward players and engage new user segments.

![](https://prod.docsiteassets.roblox.com/assets/monetization/rewarded-video-ads/playbooks/Icon-ARPU.png)

### Easy way to increase earnings

Add in Studio and Roblox handles brand demand.

![](https://prod.docsiteassets.roblox.com/assets/monetization/rewarded-video-ads/playbooks/Icon-Engagement.png)

### Improve player engagement

Rewarded Video can help boost engagement.

## Seamless integration drives engagement

Here are four best practices to ensure your rewarded video performs.

![A player choosing whether to watch a rewarded video ad](https://prod.docsiteassets.roblox.com/assets/monetization/rewarded-video-playbook/opt-in-ad.png)

### Opt-in ads

Present the reward first, followed by the option to opt in, skip, or pay.

"I'm opting in because I want the reward and that makes the experience feel more fair and less intrusive."

![A rewarded video offer at a natural break point in gameplay](https://prod.docsiteassets.roblox.com/assets/monetization/rewarded-video-playbook/moments-of-need.png)

### Moments of need

Place rewarded ads at natural break points, such as post-defeat, end-of-level, and wait screens.

"Appearing right after you lose a level or when you're out of lives feels helpful instead of forced."

![A rewarded video billboard in an experience](https://prod.docsiteassets.roblox.com/assets/monetization/rewarded-video-playbook/respect-the-player.png)

### Respect the player

Target 15 to 30 seconds for in-game rewarded video, and longer for interactive or playable ads.

"Long ads are boring. Nobody wants to watch a 2 minute ad on their phone when playing a game."

![An interactive branded space inside an experience](https://prod.docsiteassets.roblox.com/assets/monetization/rewarded-video-playbook/interactivity.png)

### Interactivity

Where possible, invest in interactive elements to better meet the needs of the Roblox audience.

"I also like that it's interactive, so I'm not just sitting there watching, I feel more involved and less distracted."

## Choosing rewards

Offer meaningful rewards, such as boosters, in-game currency, extra lives, and temporary privileges.

## Rewarded Video settings to make it safe

### Protect spenders

Hide rewarded video from users likely to purchase, which protects purchase revenue. See [Exclude likely spenders](/docs/production/promotion/rewarded-video-ads#exclude-likely-spenders) .

### Test

Use creator experimentation to measure impacts to key game metrics. See [De-risk your implementation with experiments](/docs/production/promotion/rewarded-video-ads#de-risk-your-implementation-with-experiments) .

## Types of Rewarded Video

![A Join with reward prompt on an experience details page](https://prod.docsiteassets.roblox.com/assets/monetization/rewarded-video-playbook/join-with-reward.png)

### Join with Reward

An ad placement feature that lets players get a reward before they enter the experience.

![A rewarded video billboard offering free items](https://prod.docsiteassets.roblox.com/assets/monetization/rewarded-video-playbook/rewarded-video-billboard.png)

### Rewarded Video Billboard

Easy-to-integrate templates that you drag and drop into your game.

![A free item offer with a watch ad button in an experience HUD](https://prod.docsiteassets.roblox.com/assets/monetization/rewarded-video-playbook/in-game-rv.png)

### In-game Rewarded Video

A rewarded video option that lives within the core game loop, using the HUD or in-game menus.

## Self-Audit Checklist

-

Is your RV prompt visible within the first couple of actions after the triggering moment, not buried in a menu?

-

Do you offer more than one reward type across the prompt?

-

Is your frequency cap at least 2-3 prompts per session (or an equivalent daily cap)?

-

Are your rewards consumable and repeatable rather than one-time durables?

-

Is your RV integrated within or near the core loop of the game?

Rule of thumb: diagnose from the metric that's lagging, then apply the matching fix from the [optimization playbook](/docs/production/promotion/rewarded-video-optimization)  before testing new creative or ad network changes.
