# Plane

English

[Edit](https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/blob/main/content/en-us/physics/constraints/plane.md)

For an overview on creating, visualizing, and simulating mechanical constraints, including [PlaneConstraint](/docs/reference/engine/classes/PlaneConstraint) , see [Mechanical constraints](/docs/physics/mechanical-constraints) . Also see [Roblox units](/docs/physics/units)  to understand how Roblox units compare to metric units.

A [PlaneConstraint](/docs/reference/engine/classes/PlaneConstraint)  moves its [Attachment0](/docs/reference/engine/classes/Constraint#Attachment0)  and [Attachment1](/docs/reference/engine/classes/Constraint#Attachment1)  into a position/orientation along a plane whose normal vector is the primary axis of [Attachment0](/docs/reference/engine/classes/Constraint#Attachment0) . Both parent assemblies remain free to translate and rotate unless otherwise constrained.

Compare the following examples for how the orientation of [Attachment0](/docs/reference/engine/classes/Constraint#Attachment0)  defines the plane, while the orientation of [Attachment1](/docs/reference/engine/classes/Constraint#Attachment1)  has no bearing.

Orientation = (0, 0, 90) Orientation = (-45, 0, 90)
